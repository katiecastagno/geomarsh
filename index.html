GeoMARSHbeta

Geospatial Marsh Assessment and Ranking: Sediment Health

GeoMARSH is a GIS-based data model that combines geologic and spatial data in salt marshes to score marsh suitability and prioritization, for current and future decision makers.

The layers

Using the map

Turn layers on and off with the switches, or turn on Swipe compare and drag the handle to compare any two layers side by side. Zoom in and click a grid cell to see every component score.
